The main Skill’s drafting, section, citation and template-handling methods are separated from its full ML workflow, format conversion, publication and tooling. ARIS `paper-write` is not a textual source for this implementation. No external paper, blog, conference template, style file or third-party image is vendored. The links below retain Orchestra’s attribution of methods; they are optional background, not required runtime dependencies, current venue rules, or citations to insert automatically into the user’s paper.
