# paper-writing / systems

材料以系统设计与实现为核心贡献：架构、原型、end-to-end、microbenchmark、ablation 或 scalability。用户可在 `/research-os` 选择 Systems 分支，或直接显式调用统一 `paper-writing` 并表达 Systems 专业需求；只读意图仍走 route-only。

## 统一入口

1. 读取 `skills/writing-cycle/paper-writing/SKILL.md`、`skills/writing-cycle/paper-writing/references/composition-map.md` 与 `skills/writing-cycle/paper-writing/references/standards.md`，按授权组织本次写作。
2. 读取 `skills/writing-cycle/paper-writing/references/systems-standards.md`；起草系统章节时读取 `skills/writing-cycle/paper-writing/references/systems-writing-methods.md`，核查评价面时读取 `skills/writing-cycle/paper-writing/references/systems-evaluation-methods.md` 与 `skills/writing-cycle/paper-writing/references/systems-checklist.md`，指定 venue 或评审时读取 `skills/writing-cycle/paper-writing/references/systems-venue-and-reviewer.md` 并核对当期官方规则。缺少系统证据时保留 `MISSING SCALABILITY EVIDENCE` 等原标签，不把缺失数据写成结果；依赖缺口的主张不得标为 `submission-candidate`，就绪口径遵循评价方法中的证据面与独立审查条件。交付报告时使用 `skills/writing-cycle/paper-writing/templates/systems-paper-writing-report.md`。写入范围和预算沿用委托，补实验仍须相应运行授权。
3. 仅组合当前任务适用的 `paper-plan`、`paper-drafting`、`paper-compile`、只读审查与其他内部能力；复用 `paper-writing` composition map，不复制成平行流程。Systems 证据缺口保持 `MISSING SCALABILITY EVIDENCE` 等原标签，不把缺失数据写成结果。

## 停止

遵循统一 `paper-writing` 入口的交付与停止条件；不启动投稿、上传或未授权实验。
